# Upstream attribution

FA Target 1.0 is derived from `overextended/ox_target` 1.18.1 under the MIT License.
The original copyright and license are preserved in `LICENSE`.

FA Target adds a new React/TypeScript interface, Focus and Classic presentation modes,
option keybinds and hold actions, action feedback, theme persistence, and compatibility
aliases for ox_target, qb-target and qtarget.
